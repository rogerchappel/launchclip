# LaunchClip 0.2.0

**Describe your video. Drop in your stuff. Get the finished video.**

This is the first release built for founders launching a product. One prompt, plus whatever you have (a logo, a product demo, screenshots, a talking-head clip, a voiceover), becomes a fully edited, designed, scored and captioned video.

## What's new

- **One prompt to a finished video**, through the AI agent you already pay for (Claude Code, or Codex on a ChatGPT plan) with the LaunchClip skills, or headless with `launchclip make`.
- **No API keys needed.** The voice, music, sound effects and rendering are built in and run on your machine. ElevenLabs, OpenAI and Anthropic keys are optional upgrades.
- **It decides what to make:** a motion-graphics explainer, a voiceover-led piece, or an edit over your footage. Landscape or portrait, from a 15-second Short to a couple of minutes.
- **Demo autopilot:** point it at your SaaS codebase and it starts the app, records the key flows on safe demo data, and edits them in with zooms and callouts timed to every click.
- **Raw footage, cleaned up:** silences, bad takes and filler words removed, and the voice denoised, de-hummed, de-essed and levelled.
- **Your style:** five starter styles, styles drafted from your previous videos, 14 caption styles, motion grammars, a world camera and ambient motion.
- **Change it by chatting:** only the changed scenes re-render.
- **Finish in your editor:** export to Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or OpenTimelineIO.

## Install

```bash
npm install --global launchclip
launchclip skills install --agent claude    # or: --agent codex
```

Then ask your agent: `/launchclip-create-video-remotion Make a 60-second landscape launch video for my product using the files in ./assets`

Requirements: Node.js 22+ and FFmpeg. The first run downloads the renderer's browser and the offline voice model (about 325 MB).
