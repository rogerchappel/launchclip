# Frame: Warm Retro-Computer Editorial

## Format and intent

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps.
- Duration: 90 seconds, voiceover-led, one legible idea per shot.
- Creative premise: an early-1980s home-computer manual collides with a refined editorial infographic.
- The reference is quality pressure only. Do not reuse its wording, masthead, exact scene order, icons, layouts, graphics, audio, or brand treatment.

## Palette

- Paper: `#EFE8DF` — dominant warm ivory field.
- Ink: `#171510` — primary copy and diagrams.
- Cartridge orange: `#CB5C32` — emphasis, checkpoint numbers, active progress.
- Moss phosphor: `#57664F` — proof chips, positive states, secondary data.
- Clay shadow: `#D6C6B9` — borders, inactive tracks, quiet depth.
- Deep brown: `#563324` — secondary labels and warm dark accents.
- The palette should feel related to the reference without reproducing its exact compositions.

## Typography

- Headline: an editorial serif with blunt, confident weight; large enough for a phone at arm's length.
- Emphasis: italic serif in cartridge orange, used for one phrase rather than whole sentences.
- System labels: uppercase monospaced text with generous tracking.
- Numeric readouts: heavy monospaced or slab figures with tabular alignment.
- Use semantic headlines and stat chips, not full-transcript captions.

## Persistent frame language

- A tiny monospaced status line sits in the top safe area: `BONSAI 27B · LOCAL MODE`.
- A one-pixel progress rail runs along the bottom and advances continuously.
- Very faint, oversized background motifs drift slowly: cartridge silhouettes, binary tiles, memory blocks, and diagonal speed marks.
- Keep abundant negative space. Protect every headline and number from decorative overlap.

## Visual sequence

1. Boot screen: a phone silhouette powers on and reveals `27B PARAMETERS` as a boss-level title.
2. Memory map: a 54 GB block stack collapses into a 3.9 GB cartridge; the change must be spatial, not merely typographic.
3. Binary mechanic: a field of full-precision values snaps into `−1 / +1` tiles and packs into a tiny ROM.
4. Checkpoint one: a memory meter drains by fourteen segments and locks green.
5. Checkpoint two: a compact arcade score table compares `85.0`, `80.5`, and `76.1`; explain the quality/size trade-off visually.
6. Checkpoint three: three device lanes race to `11`, `87`, and `163 tok/s` without implying cross-device equivalence beyond reported throughput.
7. Capability map: image, tool, reasoning, and long-context modules plug into one local-device bus.
8. End card: `INTELLIGENCE / GIGABYTE` becomes the high score, then the final trade-off question appears.

## Motion physics

- Use crisp 8-bit-inspired transitions—pixel wipes, scanline reveals, cartridge clicks, number rolls—but render typography and curves cleanly at full resolution.
- Main elements enter with fast acceleration and short, damped settling; no elastic toy bounce.
- Background motifs drift continuously at low contrast to prevent dead frames.
- Prefer shared-object handoffs: the 3.9 GB cartridge should persist from the memory shot into the quality and speed checkpoints.
- Shot cadence should average 7–11 seconds with internal reveals every 1.5–3 seconds.
- Avoid rapid hard cuts, generic slideshow fades, and constant full-frame camera motion.

## Audio direction

- ElevenLabs instrumental music, about 114 BPM.
- Retro computer-game feel: restrained square-wave arpeggio, warm FM bass, muted 8-bit percussion, occasional coin/checkpoint accents, and a subtle rising level-complete motif.
- Modern mix discipline: leave a broad midrange pocket for narration; no vocals, no lead melody competing with speech, no harsh bit-crushed master, no comedy or novelty-game tone.
- SFX should correspond to visible events: boot chirp, cartridge latch, memory collapse, three checkpoint locks, score roll, and final confirm tone.

## Forbidden motifs

- No cyan-on-black hacker aesthetic.
- No generic blue-purple gradients, floating glass cards, fake 3D devices, or copied Apple UI.
- No copied PrismML masthead, CTA, sequence, or reference-video typography lockups.
- No full-screen paragraph cards and no karaoke captions.
